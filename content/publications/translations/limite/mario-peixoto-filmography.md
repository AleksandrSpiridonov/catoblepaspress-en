# Mário Peixoto: Filmography and Bibliography

_Translated from Portuguese. Page 11 of **Cine-Clube**._

## Director

- _[[publications/translations/limite/index|Limite]]_ (1929–1930).
- _Onde a Terra Acaba_ (1932; _Where the Earth Ends_) — unfinished; originally titled _O Sono Sobre a Areia_ (_Dream over the Sand_).

## Story Writer

- _Onze Almas_ (_Eleven Souls_).
- _A Cidade na Lama_ (_The City in the Mud_).
- _Um Pássaro Triste_ (_A Sad Bird_).
- _A Alma Segundo Salustre_ (_The Soul According to Salustre_).

## Poetry

- _Mundéu_ (1931).
- _Poemas de Permeio com o Mar_ (_Poems Interspersed with the Sea_; unpublished).

## Novels

- _O Inútil de Cada Um_ (_The Uselessness of Each One_).
- _Sombrio_ (_Gloomy_; unpublished).

Contributed anonymously to Jonald’s _Estrela da Manhã_ (_Morning Star_).
