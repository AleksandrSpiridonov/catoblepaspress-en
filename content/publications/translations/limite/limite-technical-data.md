# _[[publications/translations/limite/index|Limite]]_: Technical Details

_Translated from Portuguese. Page 12 of **Cine-Clube**._

- Direction, production, story, screenplay, and editing: **Mário Peixoto**.
- Assistant directors: **Brutus Pedreira** and **Ruy Costa**.
- Cinematography: **Edgar Brasil**.
- Assistant cinematographer: **Ruy Santos**.
- Cast: **Iolanda Bernardes, Tatiana Rey, Olga Breno, Carmen Santos, Mário Peixoto, Brutus Pedreira, Raul Schnoor**.
- Filmed in Mangaratiba.
- 1929–1930.
