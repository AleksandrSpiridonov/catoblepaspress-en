## Translation competition

> [!info] Submissions are closed
> The competition ran until 31 July 2026. Follow the results and new projects on the [Catoblepas Press Telegram channel](https://t.me/catoblepaspress).

Catoblepas Press held a competition to translate the French satirical and libertine poet Claude Le Petit, a contemporary of Louis XIV.

We prepared a French-language edition of _Le Bordel des Muses_ and invited participants to translate eight poems—one poem, several, or the complete cycle. Following the competition, the press plans to publish a small collection of translations.

Submissions closed on 31 July 2026.
