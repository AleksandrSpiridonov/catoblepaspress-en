On February 22, 2020, in Saint Petersburg, together with [[evglebedev|E. A. Lebedev]], we held a 26-hour performance lecture "Ecological Poetry Writing".

## Topics
- Was Saussure right?
- Where is Kholshevnikov looking?
- Tectonic plates of meter: Gasparov and fisheries.
- Congruence between poem and author, author and reader, reader and megapolis resident.
- The art of arriving late: visiting the Guermantes.
- Immortality — what is it?
- Dante is here too: is folk eloquence ecological, and why should we care?
- From Bulgarin to Bespalov — a history of asphyxia.
- From Odysseus to Ulysses. A boring voyage.
- Class betrayal, or how to saddle the world-historical spirit.
- Salvation. The anthropological factor.
