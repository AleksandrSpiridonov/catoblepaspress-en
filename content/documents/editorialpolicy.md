## Editorial Policy of Catoblepas Press and Journal

The editorial team of Catoblepas Press and Journal considers material for publication when it complies with the ethical and legal standards of the Russian Federation and international law. We do not accept material that calls for violence, promotes suicide or extremism, violates human rights and freedoms, or otherwise contravenes applicable law.

The editorial team is not responsible for the content of published contributions by individual authors. However, if a serious legal or ethical violation is identified, we will take measures to correct or remove the publication.
