_Revision dated 23 August 2026._

## 1. Analytics

1.1. This website uses Plausible Analytics to measure aggregate traffic.

1.2. According to [Plausible’s Data Policy](https://plausible.io/data-policy), Plausible does not use cookies, browser cache, or local storage for analytics; does not create persistent visitor identifiers; and does not store raw IP addresses or full User-Agent strings.

1.3. Plausible records aggregate information such as visited pages, referral sources, browser, operating system, device type, and approximate location. The data is not used by Catoblepas Press for personalized advertising or cross-site profiling.

1.4. Because the website’s Plausible integration does not place analytics cookies, the site does not display a separate consent banner for Plausible Analytics.

## 2. Embedded external services

2.1. Some pages contain embedded Google Forms or Telegram material. When these embeds load, the relevant service may receive an IP address, browser and device information, and may use its own cookies under its own policies.

2.2. Catoblepas Press does not control cookies placed by Google or Telegram. Visitors can restrict third-party cookies through their browser settings.

## 3. Managing browser data

3.1. Cookies and local-storage data can be viewed, restricted, or deleted through the browser settings.

3.2. More information about data submitted through forms appears in the [[privacy|Privacy Policy]].

3.3. The current version of this document is available at [en.catoblepaspress.ru/documents/cookies](https://en.catoblepaspress.ru/documents/cookies).
