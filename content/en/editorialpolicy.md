## Editorial policy of Catoblepas Press and Magazine
Catoblepas Press considers submissions that comply with the ethical and legal standards of the Russian Federation and international law. We do not publish materials that incite violence, promote self-harm, extremism, discrimination, or any other content that violates applicable legislation or fundamental human rights.

The editorial board is not responsible for the opinions expressed in published works. Nevertheless, we reserve the right to remove or amend publications if severe breaches of the law or ethical norms are identified after publication.
