## Who We Are
Catoblepas Press is an independent experimental publishing house founded in 2024 by [[asp|Alexander Spiridonov Jr.]]. We collaborate with artist [[medvedevartist|Evgeny Medvedev]] to publish poetry [[published/index|books]] and curate the Catoblepas [[index 1|magazine]] as a platform for literature, art, and critical theory.
