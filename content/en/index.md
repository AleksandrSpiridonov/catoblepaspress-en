## About
Catoblepas is an independent experimental publishing house founded in 2024 by [[asp|Alexander Spiridonov Jr.]]. We publish poetry [[published/index|books]] designed by artist [[medvedevartist|Evgeny Medvedev]] and curate the eponymous [[index 1|journal]] of our collective.
