Catoblepas is an independent press for contemporary poetry, a stochastic journal, and a community of cultural projects from Saint Petersburg.

## What We Do

- **[[published/index|Publish books]]** — contemporary poetry and experimental literary projects
- **[[journal/index|Publish a journal]]** — poetry, prose, essays, visual art, short films, and, of course, music
- **[[projects/index|Build communities]]** — the [[bookclub|Book Club]] and [[filmclub|Film Club]], competitions, performance lectures, and interdisciplinary projects
- **[[interviews/index|Publish interviews]]** — conversations with authors, artists, and participants in our projects

The press was founded in 2024 by [[asp|Aleksandr Spiridonov Jr.]]. Learn more [[about|about the press and its editorial team]].
